ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why This Archive?

Regional artworks are not only visual objects. They can also preserve information about communities, environments, histories, beliefs, techniques and cultural practices.

The archive is designed as a student Digital Humanities project. Each item can be described through metadata such as state, region, artist, period, material, technique, theme and source.
